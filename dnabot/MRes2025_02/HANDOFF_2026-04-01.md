Clip template status:
- Main file worked on most heavily:
  - `template_opentrons_scripts/1_UNI_clip_template_APIv2_21_final.py`

Clip template improvements completed:
- Fixed Flex pipette naming to match configured instrument names.
- Added minimum volume validation:
  - linker premix must be at least `20 uL`
  - part premix must be at least `10 uL`
- Premix and clip-mix helpers were added/refined:
  - `get_mix_repetitions(volume)`
  - `get_premix_dispense_height(volume)`
  - `perform_premix(...)`
  - `perform_clip_mix(...)`
  - `pre_wet(...)`
  - `pick_up_tip_with_reload(...)`
- `perform_premix(...)` now:
  - aspirates from low in the well
  - uses volume-dependent dispense height
  - alternates left/right x-offset during mixing
  - keeps a slower finishing pass
- `perform_clip_mix(...)` now:
  - keeps repetition-based mixing
  - alternates left/right x-offset during repeated dispenses
- `pre_wet(...)` now:
  - uses a fixed internal `2 uL`
  - includes `protocol.delay(seconds=0.5)`
- All source-well aspirations for prefix, suffix, and part transfers now:
  - pre-wet first
  - aspirate slowly from the source well
  - wait `0.5 s` after aspiration
- All destination-well dispenses for prefix, suffix, and part transfers now:
  - wait `0.5 s` after dispense before mixing

Tiprack handling:
- The clip template no longer hard-fails when more tips are needed than fit on deck.
- It now loads as many tipracks as the candidate slots allow.
- When those run out, `pick_up_tip_with_reload(...)` pauses the run and asks the user to refill the loaded tipracks, then resets tip tracking and continues.

Clip workflow notes:
- For a standard 96-well clip plate, the practical destination limit is 96 clip reactions.
- Example tip count discussed:
  - 96 clips with part premixing for 96 parts and 40 unique linkers = 520 tips total.
